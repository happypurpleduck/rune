- **Linux: a window opened with `App::float_on_open` keeps its fixed size until the compositor has shown its first frame.** The limits used to be released as soon as the GPU had finished the first frame. Hyprland holds a commit until its buffer's acquire point signals but takes `set_min_size` and `set_max_size` as they arrive, and that same finished frame wakes both it and the app, so the released limits could apply with the first commit and the window tiled instead of floating. Now they go with the paint after the first frame's callback, which comes only once that commit has applied. A window that gets no frame callbacks, such as one on a hidden workspace, keeps its fixed size until it is shown.
