- **Linux: a window opened with `App::float_on_open` keeps its fixed size until the compositor has shown its first frame.** The limits used to be released right after the first frame was presented, about 50 µs after its commit. Hyprland holds a commit until its buffer's fences signal, but takes `set_min_size` and `set_max_size` as they arrive, so the released limits could apply with that first commit and the window then tiled instead of floating. Now they go with the paint after the first frame's callback, which comes only once that commit has applied. A window that gets no frame callbacks, such as one on a hidden workspace, keeps its fixed size until it is shown.
